For more than 13 years, the Open Science Framework (OSF) has served as public-good infrastructure for research. Created in 2012 to advance openness, integrity, and trustworthiness in research, OSF has grown alongside the open science movement and become part of research workflows used by communities around the world. Our commitment to that purpose remains unchanged.
