The Tanenbaum Open Science Institute (TOSI) and the Center for Open Science (COS) have partnered to bring the Metascience 2027 conference to Montreal, Canada on June 14–16, 2027 . Metascience 2027 is a global gathering for knowledge sharing, community building, and opportunities to define a roadmap of research and intervention priorities to improve science.
