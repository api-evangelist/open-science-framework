Why reproducible manuscripts? In theory, reproducibility improves accountability to third party reviewers, allows researchers to more easily build on existing work, and can improve internal quality control and organization. In practice, reproducibility in practice tends to stop with the code, at which point authors take the results and manually paste it into the manuscript document.
