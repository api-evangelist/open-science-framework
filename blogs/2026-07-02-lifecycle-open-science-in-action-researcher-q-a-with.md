At the Center for Open Science (COS), we use the term lifecycle open science (LOS) to describe research with publicly accessible plans, outputs—such as data, materials, and code—and outcomes that are linked and findable. When those elements are connected across the research lifecycle, the work becomes easier for others to evaluate, reuse, and build on—not just the final paper.
