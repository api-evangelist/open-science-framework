The Center for Open Science (COS) is seeking new members for its Preregistration and Outcome Reporting Advisory Group, formerly the Preregistration Template Working Group. Terms begin in December 2026 . COS anchors its mission in lifecycle open science : research with publicly accessible plans, content (data, materials, and code), and outcomes that are linked and findable in a persistent, open location.
